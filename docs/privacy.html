Privacy Policy – MovieScout

MovieScout is an open-source application that allows users to discover, search for, and manage lists of movies and TV shows. This application leverages public APIs from TMDB, OMDb, and JustWatch to provide catalog data, ratings, and streaming availability.

Data Collection and Cloud Storage

Because MovieScout provides cross-device synchronization, we collect and store the following data on our secure cloud servers:

  • Authentication Data: When you sign in using Google, we collect basic profile information (such as your display name, email address, and profile picture/avatar) provided by Google.
  • App Data: We securely store your tracking lists (Watchlist, Rateslist), episode ratings, app preferences and more to synchronize them across multiple devices.

We use this information exclusively to authenticate you securely and synchronize your preferences. We do not sell your data or use it for advertising purposes.

Third-Party Services

MovieScout uses third-party services to fetch media content, handle cloud storage, and manage authentication:

  • The Movie Database (TMDB): Used to retrieve movie/TV metadata, posters, and details. Any actions performed while logged into TMDB directly may be subject to TMDB's Privacy Policy.
  • OMDb: Used to retrieve external ratings.
  • JustWatch: Used to provide streaming platform availability data.
  • Supabase: Used as our primary database and authentication backend to securely store your user profile, ratings, and lists in the cloud.
  • Google Firebase / Google Sign-In: Used for authentication. Your use of Google Sign-In is subject to Google's Privacy Policy.

User Rights and Data Deletion

You have the right to request the deletion of your account and associated data at any time. If you wish to delete your data from our servers, you may disconnect your account within the app or contact the maintainers directly.

Disclaimers

This product uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB. Streaming availability data is provided by JustWatch.
